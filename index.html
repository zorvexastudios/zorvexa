GAMEPLAY RULES

How P & N Deduction Works

01 // DECODING 'P' AND 'N' CLUES

• N (Number): How many digits in your guess exist inside the secret number (in ANY position).
• P (Position): How many of those correct digits are standing in the EXACT right position.

Secret = 4 8 2  |  Guess = 4 2 9  →  P: 1  |  N: 2
'4' and '2' are both in the secret (N=2), and '4' is in the exact 1st spot (P=1).

02 // INTERACTIVE 0–9 SCRATCHPAD (TAP TILES)

Tap digits on the in-game color bar to mark your deductions: White (Untested) → Teal (Confirmed) → Pink (Eliminated). Try tapping below:

4 1 9 8 2

📌 Pinned Solution Slots: Lock confirmed digits into the top [?] boxes to auto-fill every future guess!
